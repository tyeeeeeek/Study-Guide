How this study guide works

  • Pick Core 1 or Core 2 — questions are pulled only from that exam's domains.
  • Fixed-Length Test runs through the entire question bank once, in a new random order, then scores you.
  • Continuous Practice has no set length — it keeps serving freshly randomized questions (cycling back through the bank in a new shuffled order each lap) until you click Finish & See Results, whenever you're ready to stop.
  • Select an answer, then click Check Answer to see if you're right or wrong.
  • Wrong answers are highlighted in red, and the correct answer is highlighted in green, with a short explanation.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: press 1–4 to select an answer, Enter to check or move on.
  • At the end, you'll get a full score breakdown by domain and a list of recommended study focus areas, plus a review of every question you missed.
